How we work

The stress gets absorbed before show day.

Low stress isn't a mood - it's a schedule. Most of the work happens before anyone's watching, so show day is just execution.

01 / ADVANCE

Plan the run of show

We build the timeline, patch list, and cue sheet with your team well ahead of the date, so nothing is being figured out live.

02 / SHOW DAY

Load in, check, run

Gear is checked against the plan before doors open. The crew runs the show quietly, and adjusts without anyone in the room noticing.

03 / WRAP

Strike and debrief

Load-out, a short debrief on what to keep or change, and recordings or stream archives delivered - closed out, not left open.
